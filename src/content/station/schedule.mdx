The day starts from a **set playlist** the operator defines. That list is the spine. Listener requests go into a **queue**. They are not next, and they are not a time you pick.

We do not publish an advance timed playlist — no “this exact song at 9:00.” What you get on the listen page is now playing and recently played. The desk can see more.

Rules encoded in the software:

- earliest play is at least one hour after the request
- in any rolling 3-hour window: max 3 tracks from one album (max 2 consecutive), max 4 from one featured artist (max 3 consecutive)
- queue insert and AutoDJ pick both refuse violations

Live365 will host the licensed stream (library upload + their playlist/Event UI, or an Icecast source they relay). This site exports JSON/M3U as an operator checklist. Live365 does not import M3U today.
